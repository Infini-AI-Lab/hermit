The raw record is mounted read-only at /evidence: per question, the
complete public record — every command and tool call with its output,
the messages, the final answer, the verdict — one directory per block.
The prompt carries each question's result and the record's address.
Cite by /evidence path.

Record addresses — used in `derived_from:` lines and briefing citations —
take the form of the question id (`q17`, or `q17, q26` for several); a
host's wm recall of an id returns the worked example, its wm trace the
raw record. Question ids may appear in memory entries and cases.

A tool observation longer than 2,000 characters is cut there in the
record; a line at the cut says how many characters were left out.
