It is a corpus of 1,435 U.S. federal receipts-and-expenditures reports
(1793 to 2024): the Combined Statements of Receipts, Outlays, and Balances
of the United States Government and the earlier Congressional serial-set
receipts documents, one parsed text file per report, behind a graded
question task: each question asks for a figure or a computation from those
documents. Nothing in the corpus is indexed by question; a full-text index
over the documents is the only lookup.

A correct answer scores 1 (official scorer, numeric tolerance 0); a wrong,
empty or over-long answer scores 0. The score is accuracy alone; search
count, tool calls and time do not enter it.
