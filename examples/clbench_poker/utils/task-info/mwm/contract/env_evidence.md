The raw record is mounted read-only at /evidence: per hand, the complete
  turn record — the hole cards, the board as it ran out, every action by
  both sides in order, the opponent's cards IF the hand reached showdown
  (a hand that ended in a fold does not reveal the opponent's cards; only
  showdown records do),
  and the chip delta — one directory per block. The prompt carries
  each hand's record and its address. Cite by /evidence path.

Record addresses — used in `derived_from:` lines and briefing citations —
take the form of the hand number (`h214`, or `h214, h377` for several);
hands are numbered in the record. The opponent's name appears in every
hand header and in every record.

A tool observation longer than 2,000 characters is cut there in the record; a line at the cut says how many characters were left out.
